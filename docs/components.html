Svelte samples here are Svelte 5. Svelte 4 uses the same components with let and on:click. The use:useGlass action works in both versions. See examples/svelte/src/Svelte4.svelte.

        
Vue named slots use <template #name>. #trigger on GlassMenu, #icon and #trailing on GlassButton, and #icon and #shortcut on GlassMenuItem. The wrapper stamps those onto the custom element. HTML, React, and Svelte keep slot="trigger".
